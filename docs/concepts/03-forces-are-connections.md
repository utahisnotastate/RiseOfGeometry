# 03 · Every Force Is a Twist in Geometry

> Electromagnetism and the nuclear forces are geometry too. They're the twisting of an invisible "internal" space attached to every point.

**Status:** ✅ Established (gauge theory, 1918–1975; experimentally confirmed through the Standard Model).

---

## What everyone wishes they'd noticed sooner

Once Einstein showed gravity was geometry, physicists spent decades looking for a way to make electromagnetism geometric too. It turned out the answer had already been written down, in another department.

In 1975, Tai Tsun Wu and C. N. Yang published a "dictionary" showing that **gauge theory** (the language of the Standard Model) and **fiber bundles** (a branch of pure geometry) were *the same mathematics under different names*:

| Physicists said | Mathematicians said |
|---|---|
| gauge potential $A_\mu$ | connection |
| field strength $F_{\mu\nu}$ | curvature |
| gauge transformation | change of local frame |
| magnetic monopole | nontrivial bundle |

Yang later said he was astonished that mathematicians had "dreamed up these concepts out of nowhere." Every fundamental force we know is the **curvature of a connection**.

## Explain it to a kid

Stand facing north holding an arrow pointed straight ahead. Walk in a big triangle on a globe, down to the equator, along it, and back up, keeping the arrow pointed "straight" the whole time, never turning it. When you get back to where you started, **the arrow is pointing a different way!**

Nobody turned it. The curved surface did. How much the arrow turns tells you how curved the ground is.

Forces work the same way, except the "arrow" lives in a hidden direction inside every particle (its quantum phase). An electron that loops around a magnetic field comes back with its hidden arrow turned. That turning *is* electromagnetism.

## Examples you already know

- **Foucault's pendulum.** In museums, the pendulum's swing slowly rotates over a day. That's the arrow-on-a-globe effect caused by Earth's curvature and rotation. It's geometric phase you can watch.
- **A falling cat.** A cat with zero angular momentum can still flip over to land on its feet by changing its shape in a loop. That's a geometric phase in the space of the cat's shapes (studied by Montgomery, 1993).
- **Parallel parking.** You can't slide a car sideways, but a loop of forward/back plus steering moves you sideways. Engineers call this a "holonomy," and it's the same mathematics as gauge fields.

## The grown-up version

A charged quantum particle moving around a closed loop $C$ picks up a phase

$$
\Delta\varphi = \frac{q}{\hbar}\oint_C \mathbf{A}\cdot d\boldsymbol{\ell} = \frac{q}{\hbar}\,\Phi_B ,
$$

where $\Phi_B$ is the magnetic flux through the loop. This is the **Aharonov–Bohm effect** (1959). Remarkably, the electron is affected even when it only travels where the magnetic field is **zero**. Only the potential $\mathbf{A}$ is nonzero there. Tonomura's 1986 experiment confirmed it using electron holography around a shielded toroidal magnet.

The lesson is that **potentials are more fundamental than fields** in quantum mechanics. That's the correct, mainstream kernel of a lot of fringe "scalar potential" talk.

### Whittaker's 1903–1904 papers (what they really say)

E. T. Whittaker showed that any electromagnetic field in free space can be written using just **two scalar functions**, and that solutions to the wave equation can be built from superpositions of plane waves. This is correct, elegant mathematics. It is used today in the Debye/Whittaker potential methods of antenna and scattering theory.

It is a *representation*, like writing a vector in different coordinates. It does not predict new "longitudinal scalar waves" carrying energy or information in free space, and none have ever been measured.

## Where the original notes went too far

- ✅ "Potentials matter, not just fields": **true** (Aharonov–Bohm).
- ✅ "Whittaker decomposed EM fields into scalar potentials": **true as mathematics.**
- ❌ "Scalar potential waves can be used for weapons, mind links, or free energy": **no evidence**, and it contradicts Maxwell's equations as tested to extreme precision.
- ❌ "Force is $[M/T^2][L/T^{(2-a)}]$": this is not a recognized formula. The units don't come out to newtons for general $a$.

## Try it

- Find a Foucault pendulum at a science museum (or watch one online). At latitude $\lambda$, the swing plane turns $360°\sin\lambda$ per day. In Chicago (42° N) that's about 241° per day.
- **Arrow-on-a-ball experiment:** Draw a triangle from the pole to the equator and back on a ball. Carry a pencil around it "without turning" it. For a triangle covering 1/8 of the sphere, the pencil turns exactly 90°.
