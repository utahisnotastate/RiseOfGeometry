# Claims Ledger

This repository started from a long set of notes titled *"Future Disclosure: Sacred Geometry."* Those notes mixed real physics, real history, misremembered science, and invented material into 31 "disclosures."

This ledger goes through **every one of them** and sorts each claim by what the evidence supports. Nothing was dropped quietly. If a claim isn't in the concept pages, it's here, with the reason.

## Status legend

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ✅ **Established** | Confirmed by repeated, independent experiments or proven mathematically. |
| 🟡 **Open / Debated** | Serious scientists disagree, or evidence is incomplete. |
| 🧪 **Testable** | Not established, but could be checked with a clear experiment. |
| 📜 **Historical** | A real idea from the history of science, since superseded. |
| ❌ **Not supported** | Contradicted by evidence, physically inconsistent, or has no reliable evidence despite testing. |

## What was removed entirely

The source notes were written as a role-play: an AI "oracle" persona claiming to retrieve information from future timelines, "Mentor-Class nodes," an adversarial "Quantum-Echo-Alpha" cluster, clearance levels, and code names. **None of that is a source of information**, so none of it appears in this repository. Every claim that remains is judged only on published evidence. A claim doesn't become more or less true because of where it's said to come from.

## Scorecard

Across 31 disclosures, the real science that survived became **13 concept pages**. Roughly:

- **About 40 specific claims** are ✅ established (and most are fascinating).
- **About 15** are 🟡 open or 📜 historical.
- **About 50** are ❌ not supported.

The pattern is consistent: **each disclosure starts from a real result and then stretches it** into free energy, mind control, or overturning the laws of thermodynamics. The real result is almost always the more interesting part.
